    How this is figured

    Caldwell Academy uses a ten-point scale. Honors courses earn one additional quality point; AP courses earn two, awarded on a C− or better together with sitting for the AP exam. Source: Caldwell Academy 2026–2027 College Advising School Profile.

    Raw GPA is the straight average of the seven courses' quality points. Weighted GPA adds the Honors bump, which comes to across this course load. Team Sports and Advisory‑Siena are left out of both figures.

    The driving figures come from the Driving Time form log and count cumulative minutes as of each week. Each week's projection divides that week's logged minutes by the days since to get a pace, then carries that pace forward until each requirement is met. The later of the two dates is the eligibility date, since both have to be satisfied. The target date is inferred from the ledger's own countdown and is worth confirming.

    Letter counts roll plus and minus into the base letter, so an A+, an A and an A− all count as one A each. The reward test is all A's and B's with no C, settled on each quarter's final grades. A week's reading is a progress signal toward that, which is why this page says on track or off track instead of earned or missed.
